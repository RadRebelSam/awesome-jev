# Brand book: AwesomeJev (generated 2026-10-14)

One reference for how the AwesomeJev brand is used on the web, in print and on social. Colours are read from the logo files; every ratio below is measured, not estimated.

## Logo

- Primary mark: `logo-mark.svg`
- All logo files: `logo-1024.png`, `logo-512.png`, `logo-mark.svg`, `source/favicon.svg`, `source/logo.svg`
- Clear space: keep free space around the mark equal to one quarter of the mark's width on every side.
- Minimum size: 16 px for the favicon cut, 24 px for the mark elsewhere on screen, 10 mm in print.
- Use the version that matches the ground: light mark on dark, dark mark on light. Never place the mark on a busy image.

## Logo don'ts

- Do not stretch, skew or rotate the mark.
- Do not recolour it outside the palette below.
- Do not add shadows, outlines or effects.
- Do not rebuild the mark from another font or redraw it.
- Do not place it on a background with less than 3:1 contrast against the mark.

## Colours and accessibility (measured)

| Role | Value | Measured contrast |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | `#17171A` | white on ground 17.89:1 |
| Accent (logo) | `#57C08B` | on ground 7.94:1 |
| Accent as text on a light page | `#2F8359` | 4.65:1 on white (needs 4.5:1) |
| Accent bright, on dark | `#57C08B` | 7.94:1 on `#17171A` |

Body text needs 4.5:1, large text and interface parts 3:1. Use the text-safe accent for any accent-coloured text on a light page.
